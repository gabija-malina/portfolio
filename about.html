About

Working across paint and polygons.

My name is Gabija Malinauskaitė and I am an artist working across traditional and digital mediums, exploring storytelling through painting, 3D modeling, and animation.

My artistic practice is centered around people, emotions, and the small details that make everyday moments meaningful. I am drawn to portraiture, character-driven narratives, and the atmosphere of lived-in spaces — capturing not only how something looks, but how it feels.

I began my journey in traditional art before expanding into digital creation. After studying 3D Animation at Dongseo University, I developed a passion for combining technical skills with artistic expression.

While my 3D work focuses on creating detailed, stylized worlds and assets, painting remains an important part of my creative process. Working with traditional mediums such as oil, acryl, gouache, and pastels allows me to explore color, texture, and human expression in a more intuitive way.

I am inspired by memories, nature, old photographs, and the stories hidden within ordinary moments. Whether through a painted portrait or a digital environment, my goal is to create work that feels personal, atmospheric, and emotionally resonant.

Available for commissions, collaborations, and print inquiries — see the contact page for the fastest way to reach me.
